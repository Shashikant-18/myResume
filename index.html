Few facts about earth the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical body known to harbor life. With liquid water covering ~71% of its surface, it is a dense, terrestrial planet. Earth formed roughly 4.5 billion years ago and is orbited by a single, permanent natural satellite, the Moon

About the Earth

  • Crust: A thin, solid, rocky outer layer where life exists..
  • Mantle: A thick, hot, semi-solid layer of silicate rocks and minerals
  • Inner Core: An incredibly hot, dense, solid sphere at the planet's center
  • Outer Core: A layer of liquid metal (iron and nickel) that generates Earth's magnetic field.
